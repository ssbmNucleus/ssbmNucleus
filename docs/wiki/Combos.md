# Combos

Open **Combos** in Tracker to browse sequences from your indexed replays.
Use the character, opponent, stage, length and damage filters to narrow the
list. **My combos** and **On me** use your saved connect codes.

The controls stay in place while library details arrive. Controls that need
those details become available once they load. Use **Retry details** if that
request fails. If games still need analysis, use **Analyze now**; the same
status area shows its progress. When nothing remains to analyze, it says
**Analysis complete** and hides the analysis button until new games are pending. The library total and combos-found count include
only sequences with at least four moves, across the library; they do not apply
your current character or damage filters.

## Sharing

Public combo links require at least **four moves**. Multiple hits from a
single attack count as one move. A kill does not exempt a shorter sequence.
Your local library can keep those shorter sequences for browsing and playback,
but their Share button explains why a public combo link is unavailable.

An eligible combo does not have to be uploaded already. Sharing prepares its
replay when needed, so the first share can take longer than later shares.
