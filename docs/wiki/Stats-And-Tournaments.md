# Stats And Tournaments

Tracker brings together statistics from your indexed replays and tournament
results from your linked start.gg account.

## Stats

Set your replay folders and connect codes in Settings, then let Nucleus index
your games. Stats summarizes eligible singles games from that local history.
Character and date filters change the scope of the summary.

The summary appears before rating history and other secondary information finish
loading. When you change a filter, the previous results stay visible with an
updating message until the new results arrive. If loading fails, use **Retry**;
an error does not mean your replay history is empty.

Large replay libraries still take time to summarize. The first screen and rating
history can finish at different times.

Stats and Tourneys keep their chart columns when you switch to Settings and back.

## Tourneys

Link your start.gg account in Settings to see your tournament runs. Saved results
appear immediately when available, and a fresh history sync starts showing sets
as its first page arrives. Your profile, placements and older history may finish
later. **Syncing history…** means that work is still in progress.

The totals describe the history currently loaded. If only part of your history
is available, the caption states how many recent sets it covers. Once the current
sync finishes, use **sync older sets** to extend that history.

Open a bracket or a set's replay when you want to inspect it. These views load
on demand. Replay matches depend on the games Nucleus has indexed locally, so
newly added replay files may take time to appear alongside tournament sets.
