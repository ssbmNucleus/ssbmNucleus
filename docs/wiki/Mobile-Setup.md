# Mobile Setup

Use Nucleus on your phone to browse the games you sync from desktop Tracker.
The phone app and whole-library sync come with any paid Patreon tier, from
**$5/month**. Open desktop **Settings → Cloud** and press **Link Patreon**: the
card at the top shows what your account includes and the next step. A free
Patreon membership is enough to share single replays (choose **Join**; a Follow
is not a membership).

You can also open **Mobile setup guide** in desktop **Settings → Cloud**, in the
Cloud library card.

## Set Up Your Phone

1. **Finish desktop Tracker setup.** Add your replay folders and connect codes,
   and let Nucleus index your games.
2. **Add the phone app.** Open **tracker.ssbmnucleus.net** in your phone's browser.
   Choose **Add to Home Screen** from the browser's share or menu options, then
   close the browser.
3. **Show the desktop QR code.** In desktop Tracker, go to
   **Settings → Cloud** and press **Pair your phone**.
4. **Pair inside the phone app.** Open Nucleus from your phone's Home Screen.
   Tap **Scan pairing code**, allow camera access, and scan the QR code displayed
   on your desktop.
5. **Let your games upload.** Keep desktop Nucleus open. Large replay libraries
   take time to upload; watch progress in the **Cloud library** card in
   **Settings → Cloud**. If an upload has not started, choose **Sync now**.

The desktop QR code hides after two minutes. If it disappears before you scan
it, choose **Pair your phone** again. Once paired, the phone can refresh its library
as more games finish uploading.

If your membership ends, the phone stays paired and keeps what it already has.
It says so, with a link to renew; after renewing, syncing picks up again.

## Your Cloud Activity

The Cloud activity card sits beside the Cloud library card in Settings → Cloud. It lists uploads
by date and shared replay links from your connected account, including existing
cloud history. The card initially shows up to 30 upload dates and 50 shared
replays. Use Load more to see additional entries, or Refresh to update the lists.
Recently refreshed results can take up to 30 seconds to reflect new uploads.
Select a shared replay to open it.

In the replay viewer, Randall beside W/L means the replay is synced. The share
badge opens its public replay link.
